# The Everyday Ribbed Beanie — Free Crochet Pattern

**Skill level:** Beginner — if you can chain and half-double-crochet, you can make this hat.
**Construction:** Worked flat in rows (no working in the round, no increases or decreases). You crochet a ribbed rectangle, seam one side, cinch the top closed. Done.

## Sizes

| Size | Fits head | Finished hat (circ. × height) | Foundation chain | Rows |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Toddler | 18–19″ | 16″ × 7.5″ | Ch 57 | 22 |
| Child | 20–21″ | 18″ × 8″ | Ch 64 | 24 |
| Adult M | 22–23″ | 20″ × 8.5″ | Ch 71 | 26 |
| Adult L | 23–24″ | 21″ × 9″ | Ch 75 | 27 |

Ribbing stretches, so each hat is made about 2″ smaller than the head it fits.

## Materials

- **Yarn:** Worsted weight (#4), approximately:
  - Toddler: 140 yds | Child: 170 yds | Adult M: 200 yds | Adult L: 225 yds
  - (One 200-yd skein covers Toddler through Adult M; grab a second skein for Adult L or if you add a yarn pom-pom.)
  - Full yardage by yarn weight: run your size through the free calculator at howmuchyarn.com
- **Hook:** US I-9 (5.5 mm), or size needed to obtain gauge
- Yarn needle, scissors
- Optional: pom-pom (faux fur or yarn, ~25 yds extra if yarn)

## Gauge

14 sts × 12 rows = 4″ × 4″ in half-double-crochet, back-loop-only, blocked lightly.
Check your gauge — if your rectangle is wider than the finished size, go down a hook size; if narrower, go up.

## Abbreviations (US terms)

- ch — chain
- hdc — half double crochet
- blo — back loop only
- st(s) — stitch(es)

## Pattern notes

- The turning chain (ch 1) does not count as a stitch.
- Work **every** stitch in the back loop only — that's what creates the stretchy ribbing.
- Count your stitches at the end of Row 1. Every row after that has the same count.

## Instructions

**Foundation:** Ch 57 (64, 71, 75).

**Row 1:** Hdc in the 2nd ch from the hook and in each ch across. — 56 (63, 70, 74) hdc. Count them.

**Row 2:** Ch 1, turn, hdc in the blo of each st across. — 56 (63, 70, 74) hdc.

**Rows 3–22 (24, 26, 27):** Repeat Row 2.

Your piece is a ribbed rectangle measuring 16 (18, 20, 21)″ wide × 7.5 (8, 8.5, 9)″ tall.

## Finishing

1. Fold the rectangle in half with right sides together, bringing the two short ends together. Seam the short ends with a whipstitch (or slip-stitch through both layers). Fasten off, leaving a 12″ tail.
2. Thread the tail onto your yarn needle. Weave it in and out along the entire top edge, going under one loop of every row-end. Pull tight to cinch the crown closed. Knot securely and weave in the end.
3. Turn the hat right-side out. Weave in the starting tail.
4. Fold up a 2–3″ brim if you like the cuffed look. Sew on a pom-pom if you're using one.
