How Much Yarn for a Shawl?

A medium triangular shawl takes about 700 yards of fingering-weight yarn — the classic two-skein shawl, since hand-dyed fingering often comes in 400-yard skeins. In lace weight, plan on about 900 yards; in worsted, about 450.

Shawls are the signature two-skein project: many patterns are written specifically for two skeins of fingering yarn.

Frequently asked questions

How many skeins for a shawl?

Most shawls take 1 to 2 skeins. Two 400-yard fingering skeins is the classic shawl quantity; one 800–1,000-yard lace skein also works beautifully.

How much yarn for a large wrap shawl?

Large wraps and stoles need 1,000–1,400 yards of fingering — roughly 3 skeins.

What is the best yarn weight for shawls?

Fingering (1) and lace (0) give the drape shawls are known for. DK and worsted make cozier, faster winter shawls.

Can I make a shawl from one skein?

Yes — one-skein shawlettes are a whole pattern category, designed for a single 400-yard fingering skein.
