How Much Yarn for a Crochet Market Bag?

A standard crochet market bag takes about 300 yards of worsted-weight cotton yarn — roughly 2 skeins. Cotton is the go-to fiber: strong, washable, and it doesn't stretch out of shape the way acrylic can under a heavy load.

Mesh-style market bags use less yarn than solid bags — sometimes as little as 200 yards — because the openwork stitches cover more area per yard.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best yarn for market bags?

Worsted-weight cotton or cotton blends. Cotton is strong and inelastic, so the bag keeps its shape when loaded with groceries.

How many skeins for a tote bag?

A solid tote takes about 400–500 yards of worsted (2–3 skeins). Openwork market bags take about 250–350 yards.

Will a crochet bag stretch?

Cotton bags stretch less than acrylic ones. A tight gauge and a sturdy handle attachment (worked, not sewn, where possible) keep the bag's shape.

How much yarn for a small produce bag?

About 150–200 yards of cotton — a quick project and a good use of partial skeins.
