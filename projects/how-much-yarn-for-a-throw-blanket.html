How Much Yarn for a Throw Blanket?

A standard 52″ × 60″ throw blanket needs about 2,200 yards of worsted-weight yarn — roughly 10 skeins of a typical 220-yard skein, with buffer. In bulky weight that drops to about 1,750 yards (around 15 skeins of 120-yard skeins), and in super bulky about 1,400 yards.

Chunky hand-knit throws are the fastest route: a super-bulky throw can work up in a weekend. Dense stitch patterns can add 20–30% to these estimates, so textured designs should start from the higher end.

Frequently asked questions

How many skeins of chunky yarn for a throw blanket?

For a 52″ × 60″ throw in bulky (5) yarn, plan on about 1,750 yards — roughly 15 skeins of a typical 120-yard bulky skein. In super bulky (6), about 1,400 yards, or roughly 20 skeins of 70-yard skeins.

What size is a standard throw blanket?

A standard throw is 52″ × 60″ (about 132 × 152 cm). Lapghans are smaller at around 36″ × 48″ and need roughly half the yardage of a full throw.

How much yarn for a crochet throw vs knit throw?

Crochet uses roughly 25–30% more yarn than knitting for the same size. If you crochet, add about a quarter to the knit estimates — or just use the calculator above, which builds in a safety buffer.

Can I mix yarn weights in one throw?

You can, but it changes the drape and gauge. If you substitute, match total yardage rather than skein counts: multiply the pattern's skeins by their yardage, then divide by your new yarn's yardage per skein.
