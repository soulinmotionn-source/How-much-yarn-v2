How Much Yarn for a King Size Blanket?

A king-size blanket (about 108″ × 108″) is the largest common handmade project: roughly 6,500 yards of worsted-weight yarn, or about 30 skeins of a standard 220-yard skein with buffer. Realistically, almost everyone makes bed-size blankets in bulky or super bulky — about 4,800 yards of bulky or 3,600 yards of super bulky.

Projects this size are almost always done with jumbo chenille or blanket-style yarns sold in large balls, which keeps the skein count (and the number of ends to weave in) manageable.

Typical size: King blanket: 108″ × 108″ (274 × 274 cm)

Frequently asked questions

How many skeins for a king size blanket?

In super bulky yarn, about 3,600 yards — roughly 50+ skeins of standard 70-yard skeins, which is why jumbo balls (often 200+ yards each) are the practical choice. In worsted weight it would be about 30 skeins of 220-yard skeins, a very long project.

What size should a king blanket be?

A handmade king blanket is typically 108″ × 108″. Some makers go 108″ × 96″ to save yarn — the calculator's buffer covers minor size differences.

How much does the yarn cost for a king blanket?

Easily $150–$300+ depending on the yarn. Buying during sales and choosing budget-friendly blanket yarns in large balls brings the cost down significantly.

Can I arm-knit a king blanket?

Yes — arm knitting with jumbo chenille yarn is one of the fastest ways to make a king-size blanket, often finished in a matter of days rather than months.
