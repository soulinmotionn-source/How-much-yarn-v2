How Much Yarn for a Vest?

An adult medium vest takes about 900 yards of worsted-weight yarn — roughly 5 skeins of a standard 220-yard skein with buffer. No sleeves means roughly 30–40% less yarn than a full sweater.

Vests are a smart first garment: faster than a sweater, less yarn to buy, and no sleeve island to get stuck on.

Frequently asked questions

How many skeins for a vest?

In worsted weight, about 4–5 skeins of 220-yard skeins for a medium. In DK, about 4 skeins of 280-yard skeins.

How much yarn for a sweater vest vs a sweater?

A vest uses roughly 60–70% of the yarn of a long-sleeve sweater in the same size — the missing sleeves save a lot.

Can I convert a sweater pattern to a vest?

Yes — work the body as written and skip the sleeves, binding off or working armhole edging instead. Reduce the pattern's yardage by about a third.

What yarn weight is best for vests?

DK and worsted are the sweet spot: warm enough to matter as a layer, without the bulk of heavier weights under a jacket.
