How Many Skeins of Yarn for a Sweater?

An adult medium sweater takes about 1,400 yards of worsted-weight yarn — roughly 7 skeins of a standard 220-yard skein, with buffer. In fingering weight, plan on about 2,000 yards; in bulky, about 1,050 yards.

Sweaters are where dye lots really matter: buy every skein you'll need before you start, all from the same dye lot, and alternate skeins every couple of rows for the most even color.

Frequently asked questions

How many skeins for an adult sweater?

In worsted weight: about 6–7 skeins of 220-yard skeins for a medium (with buffer); size up or down by roughly one skein per size. In fingering: about 5 skeins of 440-yard skeins. In bulky: about 9 skeins of 120-yard skeins.

How much yarn for a plus-size sweater?

Add roughly 15–25% per size above medium — a 2X–3X sweater in worsted can need 1,800–2,000+ yards. Always check the pattern's yardage for your exact size.

Does a cardigan use more yarn than a pullover?

Usually yes — the button bands and often-longer body add roughly 10–15% more yardage than a pullover in the same size.

Should I swatch before buying sweater yarn?

Absolutely. Your personal gauge can shift yardage needs by 10–20% either way, and a swatch is cheap insurance on a big yarn investment.
