How Much Yarn for a Cowl?

An average cowl (about 26″ around and 10″ tall) needs roughly 300 yards of worsted-weight yarn — comfortably 2 skeins of a standard 220-yard skein. A long double-wrap cowl needs about double: 500–600 yards.

Cowls are ideal one- or two-skein projects and a great way to try a luxury yarn without committing to sweater quantities.

Frequently asked questions

How many skeins for a cowl?

Most cowls take 1 to 2 skeins. A single 220-yard worsted skein is often enough for a snug cowl; a taller or double-wrap cowl needs 2 to 3 skeins.

How much yarn for a chunky cowl?

About 250 yards of bulky or 200 yards of super bulky — typically 2 skeins of bulky or 3 skeins of super bulky.

What is the difference between a cowl and an infinity scarf?

A cowl is usually shorter and worn close to the neck, often in the round. An infinity scarf is longer and can be looped twice. Yardage for an infinity scarf is closer to a full scarf (350–500 yards worsted).

Can I make a cowl from one skein?

Yes — one 220-yard worsted skein or one 400-yard fingering skein makes a lovely cowl. It's one of the best one-skein projects.
